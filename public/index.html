The Master Plan

This trip is more than a vacation. It's a due-diligence mission to identify, test, and potentially acquire a global family estate. By combining our resources, we can secure an asset that serves the family for generations.

How LLC Co-Acquisition Works

1. Form the Syndicate

Multiple households form a unified LLC. This entity officially purchases the property, insulating individual personal assets from liability.

2. Pool the Capital

Split five ways, a $1M estate is $200k per household and a $3M estate is $600k. Commercial mortgages can be secured jointly via the LLC; every listing shows its asking price in your chosen currency.

3. Shared Usage

Each family is allocated guaranteed weeks. Unused time is rented via luxury agencies, generating passive income.
