Generational Wealth & Co-Buying

This trip has a dual purpose: a vacation, and a due-diligence tour of real estate the family could acquire together through a Family LLC.

How it would work

  • Participating households fund an LLC. The LLC seeks a commercial or portfolio loan for the asset.
  • The property serves as a private family retreat and an international short-term rental.
  • Rental income services the debt and funds management (staff, maintenance).

Nothing here is legal, tax, or investment advice. Structure, jurisdiction, and lending terms get reviewed with a licensed professional before any commitment.
